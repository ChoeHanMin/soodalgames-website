SOLO DEV

Alone, But All In

Soodal Games has no team, and no separate game-engine license. What I have is one laptop and whatever hours I can find at night. After class I run around to extracurriculars, work out in the evening, and squeeze in time between all of that to plan the next game. Once it's past midnight I write code, and at dawn I fix the bugs I made the day before. Designer, developer, artist, sound director, QA, marketer — every role it takes to finish a game, I handle all of it myself.

"Every line of code, every piece of art, every bug fix on this site —
all made with my own blood, sweat, and tears."

— Choi Hanmin, Soodal Games

PROCESS

How a Game Gets Made

From a one-line idea to a Google Play / Steam release, in 7 steps

01
💡Concept & Idea Sketch

A habit of eyeing subway seats, a way to blow off stress, a courtroom drama I used to love — it starts by boiling some everyday spark into one line of core loop. If a single fun action isn't crystal clear, it never moves to the next step.

02
⌨️Prototype — No Engine, By Hand

No game engine — the core mechanics are written by hand in plain HTML5 / CSS / JavaScript with the Canvas API. The build stays light and testable in any browser, so I can validate whether an idea is actually fun fast.

03
🎨Art, Sound & UI

Sprites, effects, fonts, every single button, and sound effects — all picked and polished by me alone. With no team, I swap between designer, developer, artist, and sound director hats several times a day.

04
🧪Playtesting & Balancing

I run dozens, sometimes hundreds of rounds myself to tune difficulty and balance. Balancing the weapons in Stealth Ops once ran until 4AM — if it isn't fun, I tear it down and rebuild it.

05
🌐Korean/English Localization

Every game ships with Korean and English support by default, for players at home and abroad alike. No machine translation — every line of text is translated and polished by hand.

06
📦Packaging — PWA → TWA / Electron

The finished web game is wrapped as a PWA, then packaged as a TWA (Trusted Web Activity) build for Google Play and as an Electron build for Steam. I designed this structure myself so one codebase supports both platforms at once.

07
🚀Launch & Live Updates

Launch isn't the finish line. I keep stacking commits based on bug reports and reviews. You can watch that record in real time in the timeline below.
